During March, Pinhel, the leader of Team M1 on the Portuguese side, shared a document titled `Team Structure DSD.pdf` and proposed a six-team division for the wider DSD project.

## A Cross-Team Proposal

At the start of the course, most teams only had a broad understanding of what the project was about. This document pushed the discussion further by addressing a more practical question: who would do what, how teams would connect, and how parallel development could become possible.

## Why It Mattered to M2

For M2, the proposal did more than introduce an organisational idea. It helped us understand our likely place in the overall system much earlier, and gave us a clearer sense of the collaboration boundaries and dependencies that would shape our later work.
