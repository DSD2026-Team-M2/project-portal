The DSD course officially began on March 19. Members from China and Portugal joined the first video meeting to get a shared understanding of the Limb Motion Recognition and Assistant project, its collaboration model, and the initial working rhythm.

## Kickoff Meeting

This meeting was mainly about alignment rather than implementation. It gave the team a first common picture of the project goals and also made the realities of distributed work immediately visible: time zones, communication cadence, and role coordination would all shape the work ahead.

## First Leadership Decision

At this stage, detailed group responsibilities had not yet been assigned. The only role confirmed during the kickoff was the group leader, and Lee was selected to take that position and coordinate the team's early organisation and communication.

## What Comes Next

The course had officially started, but the working structure was still taking shape. The next step for M2 was to complete team formation under a clearer overall framework and begin moving from orientation into concrete work.
