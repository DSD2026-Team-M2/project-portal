On March 31, the first version of the M2 portal officially went live. With this launch, the team gained a more stable public-facing space for presentation and process tracking.

## A New Team Hub

Unlike a single page or one-off deliverable, the portal works as an ongoing team hub. It gives M2 a place not only to show pages, but also to gather records, links, intermediate outputs, and the growing documentation of the work itself.

## Why It Matters

As a project develops, what matters is not only what has been built, but also whether the process can be made understandable to others. The portal marks a shift: M2 is not only building interfaces, but also building a clearer narrative and structure around its own work.
